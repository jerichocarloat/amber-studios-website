FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All questions

Our reception is open daily from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday through Sunday. While we do not offer 24-hour reception, you can reach us outside these hours via our AI BotChat system on WhatsApp for any assistance you may need.

Please note that we operate a single reception for both locations, which is located at Amber Gardenview Studios, Veres Pálné utca 26, 1053 Budapest. When you arrive, you’ll find our doorbell labeled "Amber Studios" to the right of the entrance. Our other location, Amber Terrace Studios, is located in Veres Pálné utca 7, 1053 Budapest, just 100 meters apart and managed through a single reception.

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM at our reception, located at 26 Veres Pálné utca, 1053 Budapest at Amber Gardenview Studios. You can check in at reception between 3:00 PM and 6:30 PM for both Amber Gardenview Studios and Amber Terrace Studios.

If you plan to arrive after our reception hours, you will need to use our self-check-in service. Please email us your expected arrival time. If you are arriving outside of reception hours, we will provide you with detailed self-check-in instructions.

Please note that, in accordance with local regulations, all guests are required to upload their identification documents into our online registration system which is connected to the Hungarian VIZA system of the Ministry of Internal Affairs.

For guests with a reservation at Amber Terrace Studios (7 Veres Pálné utca), if you arrive after reception hours, you will check in directly at that location using our self-check-in process—there is no need to visit the reception at Amber Gardenview Studios.

If you arrive after 7 PM, self-check-in is required. Please inform us in advance if you will be arriving outside our reception hours. Your accommodation must be fully paid, and your documents uploaded to our online registration system, as required by law. Once your reservation is active and paid, you will have access to the registration portal. After completing registration, we will send you detailed self-check-in instructions, including how to retrieve your key from our keybox system and access your studio.

Please also note whether your accommodation is at Amber Gardenview Studios (Veres Pálné utca 26) or Amber Terrace Studios (Veres Pálné utca 7), as this is important for your arrival.

You may securely store your bicycles in the inner courtyards of Amber Gardenview Studios or Amber Terrace Studios. It is not permitted to keep bicycles inside the studios.

Please be advised that parking in Budapest’s city center on public streets is chargeable, and certain areas are strictly reserved for permanent residents. To facilitate your parking needs, we recommend using the PARKL app, available for download on both Google Play and the App Store.

Secure your parking spot in advance: we encourage you to book a parking space in one of the underground garages close (5-8 min walking distance) to our property in advance through our trusted partner budapestcarpark.com. Use our exclusive coupon code AMBER10 to receive a 10% discount on your booking.

Pollack Garage: a separate underground parking facility is available at Pollack Garage on Pollack Mihály Square, approximately a 10-minute walk from the property. This garage, which is not included in the budapestcarpark.com reservation system, is the most economical option nearby. Address: 1080 Budapest, Pollack Mihály Square.

Amber Studios operates two separate locations, each just 100 meters apart and managed through a single reception. Amber Gardenview Studios are situated at 26 Veres Pálné utca, where our reception is also located. Amber Terrace Studios can be found at 7 Veres Pálné utca, a short walk from the main building. Both properties offer Small, Medium, and Large Studios, all similarly equipped to ensure a comfortable stay. The main difference is their address and proximity to the reception; otherwise, guests can expect the same level of amenities and service at both locations.
