Directions

Getting to Veres Pálné utca

From the airport

Liszt Ferenc Airport is 22 km away. The fast airport bus 100E departs from Kálvin tér, 550 m from Amber Studios. Or book a door-to-door transfer with Welcome Pickups.

By metro

Kálvin tér (metro lines 3 and 4) is within 550 m; Ferenciek tere (line 3) is also close by. Tram 2 runs along the Danube embankment, a five-minute walk away.

By car

Street parking in the city centre is chargeable (PARKL app). Underground garages 5–8 minutes away can be booked via budapestcarpark.com with code AMBER10; Pollack Garage is the most economical option nearby.

Please note: we operate a single reception for both locations, at Amber Gardenview Studios, Veres Pálné utca 26. When you arrive, you’ll find our doorbell labeled "Amber Studios" to the right of the entrance. Amber Terrace Studios (Veres Pálné utca 7) is just 100 meters away.
