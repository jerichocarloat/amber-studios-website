We recommend that our guests plan visits to various attractions in Budapest before their arrival and, if possible, purchase tickets online in advance. Some popular sights, such as tours of the Hungarian Parliament, often sell out during the high season. A must during your visit to Budapest is certainly a boat cruise on the Danube—especially those after sunset, as the city’s unique night-time illumination is a spectacle in itself. Visiting our thermal baths is also a true bathing experience, both in winter and summer.

We recommend Hop-On Hop-Off bus tickets as an excellent way to explore all of Budapest’s major attractions. Tickets for the Hungarian Parliament can be bought at parlament.hu.

Budapest has an extensive, efficient, and affordable public transport system that includes metro lines, trams, buses, trolleybuses, suburban trains (HÉV), and even public boat services on the Danube.

Tickets/passes can be purchased at metro stations, vending machines, newsstands, and via the BudapestGO app. For comprehensive information about Budapest’s public transportation, with details on routes, tickets, airport transfers, and travel planning please check bkk.hu.

  • Avoid exchanging money at the airport, as rates are typically less favorable. Always check the current exchange rate on the official National Bank of Hungary website.
  • Be cautious of tourist traps in the city center, particularly on Váci utca. Always review recent comments about any venue you plan to visit on Google Maps.
  • In restaurants, double-check your invoice to see whether the service charge (szervízdíj) is included—each establishment handles this differently.
  • For your safety and to avoid being overcharged, we recommend using taxis that offer online booking and payment options. Be cautious when hailing taxis directly from the street, as some may charge significantly higher fares or use unreliable pricing.
