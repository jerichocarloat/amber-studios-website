Amber Terrace Studios – Large Studio

Our Large Studios, located on the first and second floor (be aware there is a Mezzanine / Half Floor) are designed to accommodate up to 4 guests and provide the ideal choice for families or small groups seeking comfort in the heart of Budapest. With a generous size of up to 32 m², these studios combine modern amenities with the unique charm of our lovingly restored 20th-century building. Each Large Studio features: A European queen-size bed (160 x 200 cm) and a double sofa bed (140 x 200 cm), a private bathroom with shower and toilet and a comfortable seating area.
