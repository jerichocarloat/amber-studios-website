Who we are

Two properties, one friendly team

We are operating two properties, Amber Terrace Studios and Amber Gardenview Studios, 100 m apart from each other, both located in Budapest downtown, in the 5th district in Veres Pálné street and classified by the Hungarian Tourism Authorities both as 3 star accommodations.

Friendliness is our highest principle and we are here to serve you and make your stay in Budapest unforgettable. Amber Studios is an open and hospitable community that strives to enrich guests staying with us in the heart of Budapest with new experiences.

Our international team with coworkers from 4 different countries is dedicated to making our guests truly feel at home with us, the management and ownership is Austrian/Hungarian.

Breakfast is available in nearby cafes. There are several restaurants, grocery stores, bars and cafés in a radius of 100 metres from the property. Luggage storage is available in case of a late check-out or early check-in.
