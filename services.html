Tourist info and tickets

We recommend that our guests plan visits to various attractions in Budapest before their arrival and, if possible, purchase tickets online in advance. Some popular sights, such as tours of the Hungarian Parliament, often sell out during the high season. A must during your visit to Budapest is certainly a boat cruise on the Danube—especially those after sunset, as the city’s unique night-time illumination is a spectacle in itself. Visiting our thermal baths is also a true bathing experience, both in winter and summer. Many of the most popular baths among tourists offer outdoor pools with cold water in the summer.

  • We recommend Hop-On Hop-Off bus tickets as an excellent way to explore all of Budapest’s major attractions at your own pace.
  • Tickets for the Hungarian Parliament.
  • Tickets for cruises, thermal baths, folklore concerts and dance performances, Jewish heritage tours, classical concerts and more.

Parking nearby

Please be advised that parking in Budapest’s city center on public streets is chargeable, and certain areas are strictly reserved for permanent residents. To facilitate your parking needs, we recommend using the PARKL app, available for download on both Google Play and the App Store.

Secure your parking spot in advance: we encourage you to book a parking space in one of these underground garages close (5-8 min walking distance) to our property in advance through our trusted partner. Use our exclusive coupon code AMBER10 to receive a 10% discount on your booking at Parking Budapest.

Pollack Garage: a separate underground parking facility is available at Pollack Garage on Pollack Mihály Square, approximately a 10-minute walk from the property. This garage, which is not included in the budapestcarpark.com reservation system, is the most economical option nearby. Address: 1080 Budapest, Pollack Mihály Square.
